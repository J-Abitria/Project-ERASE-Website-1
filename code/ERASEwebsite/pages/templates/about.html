{% trans "Our Story" %}

Project ERASE is an organization that was founded by Luis Morales Carrera in 2023. Here was his opening words about the project to the Renton community, when he was a student at Hazen High School in Renton:

Dear Renton community,

I am beyond excited to officially announce the start of Project ERASE. Project ERASE was born from a promise that a little 8-year-old boy made when he crossed the border. A promise based on the fundamental belief that every child deserves a chance at education, by having access to the educational resources needed. Which should not be limited to where they're born.

Project ERASE will be centered around teaching about the importance of having access to Educational Resources, and will come to closure by sending school supplies to children in Guatemala. More details coming soon! I hope to have the support from all those who know me and know how passionate I am about driving my community forward with me.

With care,

Luis Morales Carrera, Project Manager

We've been able to extend our support to over 100 students, and sent out over 10,000 supplies over the course of this project. We are committed to raising awareness about the importance of having access to education, and we will continue to give students the support they deserve, year after year.
